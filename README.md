<h2>Additional Work Added</h2>

<h3>Modeling a 10-Story Building with Grasshopper, ETABS and BHoM</h3>

<p> A dedicated playlist has been added demonstrating the practical application of <strong>Grasshopper</strong>, <strong>ETABS</strong>, and <strong>BHoM</strong> through the modeling of a <strong>10-story building</strong>. </p>

<p> The series presents the overall modeling workflow, including the development and refinement of the model, interaction between Grasshopper and ETABS, validation of the generated ETABS model, identification and resolution of modeling errors, and iterative updates to the workflow. It focuses on the practical aspects of working between the different platforms and demonstrates how issues encountered during the process can be investigated and resolved. </p>

<p> As the work is being developed in the context of structural engineering practice in <strong>Pakistan</strong>, the ETABS models incorporate the relevant loading considerations using the <strong>Building Code of Pakistan (BCP)</strong>, <strong>UBC 97</strong>, and <strong>ASCE</strong> provisions where applicable. These standards are used as part of the load definition and structural modeling process demonstrated throughout the series. </p>
